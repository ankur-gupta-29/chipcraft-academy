## Who I Am

Hi — I'm a self-taught digital IC design enthusiast who started this site to document my own learning journey and help others who are exactly where I was when I first searched "how to learn VLSI design."

The world of Digital IC Design — RTL, ASIC flows, STA, verification — can feel incredibly opaque from the outside. Most resources assume you already work at a chip company. ChipCraft Academy is my attempt to fix that.

## Why ChipCraft Academy?

When I started learning:

- Most tutorials were paywalled or buried in 500-page textbooks
- YouTube videos skipped the "why" and jumped straight to tool commands
- Forum answers assumed you already knew the jargon
- There was no single place that mapped out the full learning path

So I built one.

## What You'll Find Here

- **Plain-English articles** that explain concepts before showing tools
- **Curated course reviews** — I've taken (or thoroughly researched) every course I recommend
- **Honest affiliate links** — I only link courses I'd genuinely recommend. I earn a small commission at no extra cost to you.
- **PDF guides** — structured, printable references for common workflows and concepts
- **No gatekeeping** — everything on the blog is free

## Topics Covered

| Topic | What You'll Learn |
|-------|------------------|
| RTL Design | Verilog, SystemVerilog, design patterns |
| ASIC Flow | Synthesis, P&R, DRC/LVS, tapeout |
| VLSI | CMOS fundamentals, physical design |
| STA | Timing paths, constraints, setup/hold |
| Verification | UVM, assertions, functional coverage |
| FPGA | Prototyping, Vivado/Quartus basics |
